What is First-Agent?

First-Agent is your AI-powered financial assistant designed to help you understand and manage your financial activities.

+

How do I start a conversation?

Open New Chat and type your question or request into the message box to begin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about First-Agent.

01

Can First-Agent track my spending?

Yes. Your spending can be recorded in the Your Spendings section and organized by category.

02

Can I check my previous conversations?

Yes. Your previous conversations can be accessed from the History section.

03

Can I set a monthly budget?

Yes. You can set a monthly spending limit from Financial Preferences in Settings.

04

Where can I see my expenses?

Open Your Spendings to view your recorded expenses, categories and spending totals.
